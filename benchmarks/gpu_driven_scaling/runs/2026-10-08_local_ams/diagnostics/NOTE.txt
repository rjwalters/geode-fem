One-off diagnostics for the AMS iteration growth (issue #930). NOT part of the
benchmark and NOT committed code: each was a throwaway build (of commit af01160a unless
stated) with one patch applied, run once on the same loaded dev machine, then
discarded. Only the iteration counts are meaningful, except where the third
entry says otherwise.

pec.patch + pec_<variant>.stdout
  DIAG930_PEC selects which cube planes are PEC (default = y0 and y1, the
  benchmark fixture; y0 = one plane; none = no PEC; y0y1x0 = three planes).
  Question: is the growth caused by the fixture's two disconnected PEC planes
  (cf. the floating-PEC limitation in driven/solve_ams.rs)?
  Sizes n = 6, 9, 12, 15; configs direct, iterative, iterative_ams.

pi.patch + pi_<variant>.stdout
  DIAG930_PI selects the vector-nodal (Pi^T P Pi) coarse solve of the AMS
  (default = the shipped 4-sweep symmetric Gauss-Seidel; sgs16 = 16 sweeps;
  amg = smoothed-aggregation AMG; direct = exact sparse LU). The gradient-space
  solve is the exact LU in every variant.
  Question: is the growth caused by the inexact vector-nodal solve?
  Sizes n = 6, 9, 12, 15; config iterative_ams only.

smooth_weight.patch + sw_<label>.stdout / sw_<label>.time   (PR #944 review)
  Throwaway build of commit 10d5d871 (crates/geode-core/src/eigen/ams.rs is
  identical to af01160a's) in its own target directory. Two env knobs:
  DIAG944_SW=<w> overrides the AMS edge-smoother weight (the damped point-Jacobi
  sweep of the V-cycle; shipped DEFAULT_SMOOTH_WEIGHT = 0.6), and DIAG944_RHO=1
  prints, per preconditioner build, a Gershgorin upper bound and a
  power-iteration Rayleigh-quotient lower bound (after 50 to 2000 iterations)
  on the largest eigenvalue of D^-1 P, P being the real SPD proxy the V-cycle
  smooths and D its diagonal.
  Question: is the iteration growth caused by the edge smoother, the suspect
  the first two diagnostics left untested?
  All six sizes, n = 6, 9, 12, 15, 20, 24, in ONE process per label, with
  RAYON_NUM_THREADS=1, under /usr/bin/time -l (so the .time file covers the six
  sizes together, mesh generation and assembly included):
    sw_0p20 ... sw_0p65   config iterative_ams at weight 0.2, 0.3, 0.4, 0.45,
                          0.5, 0.55, 0.58, 0.6 (= shipped), 0.65
    sw_jacobi             config iterative (COCG + Jacobi): the time reference
                          from the same binary and session
    sw_rho                DIAG944_RHO=1 at the shipped weight; its setup times
                          include the 2000 power iterations and are not used
  Unlike the two diagnostics above, the per-size setup and Krylov times are
  used (single-threaded, same session as sw_jacobi), as indicative values. The
  machine load rose from about 15 to about 100 during the session: sw_0p60 and
  sw_0p65 ran at about 60% and 58% CPU, so their wall-clock times overstate the cost.
  Single runs. No weight here is a tuned or validated value, and the library
  constant was not changed.
