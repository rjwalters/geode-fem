## Critical flags (block advancement to AUDITED)

- **C1. Numerical inconsistency: space-A best designs "near 0.043F0"** (Discussion, main.tex:798-801). The Discussion says "In space A, every method's best design sits on the active mean-torque floor near $0.043F_0$, so within this budget and these five methods the parameterization and constraint, not the optimizer, set the attainable ripple." Section 7.2 (optimization.tex:137-138) and Table 7 ("best C" 0.0489) say otherwise: "The best designs of A, B, D and E all reach $0.043F_0$, and C's reaches $0.049F_0$." The raw per-method minima are A 0.04321, B 0.04320, D 0.04321, E 0.04332 and **C 0.04893**. C's best is 13% above 0.043F0. Part of the sentence is true: all five best designs lie within 0.17% of the mean floor (0.12-0.16%). But "every method's ... near 0.043F0" is false for C, and the inference "not the optimizer" is weaker for that reason, since C reaches a higher ripple on the same active floor. verify_exp2.py binds the §7.2 sentence (`rep('C','a') == .049`) but not this Discussion sentence. Suggested fix: "In space A, every method's best design sits on the active mean-torque floor, and A, B, D and E all reach $0.043F_0$ (C stops at $0.049F_0$). Within this budget, the parameterization and constraint therefore set the attainable ripple for the four SLSQP and COBYQA methods." Add a text-side assertion when fixing.
