## Critical flags (block advancement to AUDITED)

- **C1. Numerical inconsistency: "45 paired starts"** (abstract, main.tex:41). "adjoint SLSQP reached every attained ripple target sooner than COBYQA at the same training resolution in all 45 paired starts". The 45 are paired (space, target, start) comparisons: B/A gives 24 and D/E gives 21 (Table 6). They come from **five** starts per space. The body says so explicitly: "a paired case study over five starts" (optimization.tex:85-86; main.tex:791, 828). The conclusion uses the correct unit, "45 paired cases" (main.tex:825). Fix: "in all 45 paired comparisons".

- **C2. Numerical inconsistency: repeat-noise maximum "13.2%"** (optimization.tex:128). "the median difference is 2.1% and the largest is 13.2%". 13.2 is 100·|ln(t₂/t₁)| (verify_exp2.py:82-84 computes `abs(math.log(...))`), not a percentage difference. The largest pair is method D, space B, LHS 3, T99: 83.297 vs 73.002 units. That is a **12.4%** difference relative to repeat 1, or **14.1%** relative to the faster run. Neither rounds to 13.2. The median, 2.1%, holds under any definition (2.13% / 2.11%). Fix: "the largest is 12.4%" (relative to repeat 1), or say "in log ratio" and keep 13.2. Update the verifier assertion to match.

- **C3. Numerical inconsistency: conforming estimator at θ0, "2.0%" vs Table 7 "+2.1"** (optimization.tex:201-202 vs tables/exp2-refinement.tex, space-A θ0 row). "and 2.0% more at θ0". Raw values: space A **+2.070%** (Table 7: +2.1), space B +2.012% (Table 7: +2.0). The two θ0 conforming checks use different fresh meshes and differ. verify_exp2.py:132 asserts `[2.1, 2.0]` from the data, but nothing binds the sentence. Fix: "and 2.0-2.1% more at θ0".

- **C4. Linked-source contradiction: preregistration timing** (optimization.tex:4-7). "The protocol, its amendments and every later deviation are recorded in the preregistration and were fixed before any main-run outcome was observed." The linked #1185 thread dates the protocol, amendments 1-8, their addenda and the pre-main clarifications to 2026-10-17/18, each labelled "before any main run/outcome". The final-refinement deviation (comment 6284369486, 2026-10-20T05:49:54Z, operator-approved) replaced closed capture by running directly from the verified copy. It was adopted after the main session had finished and after host reboots (changelog.md, "Protocol deviation recorded before refinement"). Because the sentence's subject includes "every later deviation", it asserts something the record contradicts. The deviation leaves the cohort rule and check matrix unchanged, so this is a wording defect, not a protocol breach. Suggested fix: "The protocol and its amendments were fixed before any main-run outcome was observed; the one later deviation, which ran the final checks directly from the verified study copy, is recorded there as well."

## Non-critical notes

- **N1. "Every attained target" needs "whenever both attain"** (optimization.tex:134-136; main.tex:40-41, 823-825). In 4 repeat-1 cells COBYQA-L0 (D) attains a target that SLSQP-L0 (E) misses: space-A LHS 4 at T50 and T90, and space-B LHS 1 at T90 and T99. The text discloses this (optimization.tex:146-149; abstract "lost one start"). Still, the unqualified "at every attained target, gradient-based SLSQP reaches the target sooner" is true only for paired cells. Suggest "whenever both attain it".
- **N2. Abstract: "with SLSQP, lost one start in each design space"** (main.tex:43). In space A, E's LHS 4 run has no credited validation at all. In space B, E's LHS 1 run still attains T50 at 1.1 units; it only misses T90 and T99. Suggest "missed targets from one start in each design space".
- **N3. "Only six crossings occur after the 60-unit checkpoint"** (optimization.tex:128-130). This sentence follows "Repeat-1 times are reported", but six is the count over both repeats: three per repeat. The sets differ: B LHS 1 crosses at 59.1 in repeat 1 and 60.1 in repeat 2. Suggest "six crossings over both repeats (three in each)".
- **N4. "Within" / "at most" bounds exceeded at the third digit.** "within 2.1×10⁻¹²" (optimization.tex:190; raw 2.113e-12); "by at most 10⁻⁵" (200; raw 1.005e-5); conclusion "by at most 6.3%" (main.tex:828; raw 6.334%). Each is correct as a rounded display value, but under the bound convention of the v5/v6 audits each is narrowly false. Suggest 2.2e-12, 1.1e-5 (or "about 10⁻⁵"), and "at most 6.4%" (or "by −4.0% to +6.3%").
- **N5. Held-out paragraph wording** (optimization.tex:211-217). The 0.65-1.73 ratio is (held-out F/F₀)/(training F/F₀). A value above 1 means a *weaker* reduction, so "the reduction ranges from 0.65 to 1.73 times its value" reads inverted. Suggest "the remaining objective fraction ranges from 0.65 to 1.73 times...". Also, "In space B, the best designs lose more" holds for 4 of 5: best B's ratio is 0.89, an improvement. Say "four of the five best designs".

Section 7 charges iteration and fitting cost to the budget but reports no iteration counts or separate fitting times. Suggest "charges optimizer overhead and surrogate fitting to the budget and measures time to a validated target directly".
