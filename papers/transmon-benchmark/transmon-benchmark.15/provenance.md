markers are kept. **Symbol (v15):** the paper's island capacitance is
**C_isl**; the artifacts' field names `c_sigma_*` and the string "C_Sigma" in
them denote the same quantity (feedline floating, junction excluded). The paper's
**C_Σ = C_isl + C_J** is Koch et al.'s total. Tiers (operator ruling on #1129: no "certified"): **BOUNDED** (a two-sided bound that holds by
theorem for the meshed polygonal geometry; implementation checked by tests, not formally verified), **MODEL-DEPENDENT** (a rigorous consequence of such a bound
under a stated circuit model), **MEASURED** (a committed solve), **ESTIMATE**
(an extrapolation with a stated rule), **derived** (our arithmetic on committed
values, recomputed by `verify_evidence.py`).
