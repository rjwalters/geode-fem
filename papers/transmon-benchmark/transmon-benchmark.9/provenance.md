# Evidence for v9

v9 is v8 (`main` at `9081d1f6`) re-anchored and extended with the results
merged since v8. Evidence snapshot: `main` at `9081d1f6` (2026-10-12). No
solver or generator was run for v9. Rows of `../transmon-benchmark.8/provenance.md`
that are not listed below still describe v9's text and are not repeated
(eigenmode agreement, Palace's search window and port operator, the gauge arc,
the port-subspace update, the CPU, AMS and GPU tables, the coarse-mesh
precursor, harmonic motion); where a key named there was renamed by #1045, the
new name is the one in the artifact header's rename map.

Evidence tiers, as in v8: **CERTIFIED** (a guaranteed bound computed from a
committed artifact), **PROVEN** (a committed, regenerable measurement),
**ESTIMATE** (an extrapolation with a stated rule, not a bound), **read from
source**, **scratch probe**, **derived**, **ROADMAP**.
